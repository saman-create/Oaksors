# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Oaksors serves retirees and near-retirement investors who want to understand or begin moving eligible retirement savings into physical precious metals. Visitors need clear education, direct access to a specialist, and confidence that the process will be handled carefully.

## Product Purpose

The website explains precious-metals IRAs, presents current market education, and gives prospective clients a clear path to contact Oaksors or prepare for an account rollover. Success means a visitor understands the offer, trusts the company, and knows the next appropriate step.

## Positioning

Oaksors presents a simple, quick, honest, full-service rollover experience with hands-on paperwork, transfer, purchase, liquidation, account-management, tax, and legal support.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare retirement-diversification options, read precious-metals market news, review rollover benefits, contact customer service, and prepare personal account information. News will eventually come from a dedicated API through one reusable article contract.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, React Aria Components, and selectively adopted Untitled UI packages are the established stack.
- Existing homepage content and its native React implementation remain intact.
- Contact and onboarding forms are presentational and disabled. They must not collect, store, email, upload, or transmit data until a secure backend is explicitly provided.
- News uses a reusable listing and article template with a configurable API boundary and local development fallback.
- Existing public contact details, legal copy, and page URLs must be preserved.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Oaksors. The established site voice is direct, reassuring, and educational. “Simple. Quick. Honest.” is an existing brand promise. Existing Oaksors logos and licensed GT Standard and GT Alpina font assets must be preserved.

## Evidence on Hand

- The redesigned homepage and shared visual assets under `src/` and `assets/` are the incumbent product and design reference.
- The public Oaksors pages provide the source copy, contact information, form fields, article titles, imagery references, and privacy notice.
- No testimonials, performance claims, fabricated customer outcomes, or investment guarantees may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Make complex retirement information easy to scan without oversimplifying it.
2. Establish trust through clarity, restraint, and accurate source material.
3. Keep the next step visible without pressuring the visitor.
4. Treat financial and identity information as sensitive by default.
5. Build content structures that can grow without redesigning each new page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, responsive layouts, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast, and descriptive form status messaging.
